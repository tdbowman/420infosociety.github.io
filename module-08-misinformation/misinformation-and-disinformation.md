# Module 8: Misinformation and Disinformation 🌀

**Week 8 | Truth's Distribution Problem**

## Welcome

For seven weeks we've studied how information societies create, move, and trust information. This week we study what happens when the machinery works perfectly — and the information is false. Misinformation isn't a glitch in the information society; it's the information society's own distribution channels doing exactly what they were built to do, for content that happens to be wrong.

### 🎯 What You'll Learn

- The difference between misinformation, disinformation, and malinformation — and why intent is the hinge concept
- Why false content spreads: the emotional, social, and algorithmic forces that give falsehood a head start
- How generative AI changes the game: deepfakes, cheapfakes, synthetic propaganda, and the "liar's dividend"
- What actually works against misinformation — prebunking, labels, fact-checks, media literacy — and what the evidence says about each
- How to dissect a real misinformation event, using this week's hurricane-disinformation study as your model

### 🧠 Big Questions to Consider

- If a claim is false but the person sharing it sincerely believes it, who is responsible for the harm it causes?
- Does AI-generated propaganda change anything fundamental, or just make an old problem cheaper?
- When deepfakes are everywhere, is the bigger danger believing fake things — or refusing to believe real ones?
- Can a society correct false beliefs faster than it produces them? What would that even require?

## This Week's Journey

We start inside the misinformation machine itself: what the terms mean, why false content travels so well, and what a real disinformation event looks like up close. Then we turn to synthetic media — deepfakes, voice clones, and AI-written propaganda — and ask what changes when fabricating convincing evidence costs almost nothing. We end with the countermeasures: the full toolkit, honestly appraised, from inoculation experiments on Instagram to the limits of fact-checking.
