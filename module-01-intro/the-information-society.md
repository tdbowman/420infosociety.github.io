# Module 1: Introduction to the Information Society 🌐

**Week 1 | Information, Technology & Society**

---

## Welcome to INF 420!

This module launches the course: the syllabus, Canvas, each other — and the question that everything else this semester hangs on: *what does it mean to say we live in an information society?*

### 🎯 What You'll Learn

- Get comfortable with the course structure, this online textbook, and what's expected of you
- Define the "information society" and trace where the idea came from
- Distinguish competing meanings of *information* — and see why the definition matters
- Meet the course's cast of technologies: AI, automation, robotics, cybersecurity, and information communication
- Start practicing the course's core skill: thinking critically about claims made for (and against) technology

### 🧠 Big Questions to Consider

- What actually changed when industrial societies became information societies — and what stayed the same?
- Is "information society" a description of reality or a story we tell ourselves?
- How would you know if a forecast about AI's future was worth believing?
- Who benefits from the information society — and who gets left out of it?
